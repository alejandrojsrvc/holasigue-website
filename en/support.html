Frequently asked questions

What is included for free?

You can care for your daily base, track sessions, keep up to five active Routine plans, and save up to two reusable gym workouts. A recurring series counts as one plan, not once for every day. Saved workouts are templates: the free version does not limit you to two sessions.

What does Premium add?

Premium gives you more space for Routine plans and saved workouts, lets you import calendars, and allows you to create private Juntos groups and challenges. Editing existing plans and using reminders does not require Premium. Invited members can participate in Juntos for free.

Where can I see prices and manage my subscription?

Check current plans and terms on the purchase screen in SIGUE. The price and currency appear before you confirm; offers may vary by account. Premium is linked to your SIGUE account. You can restore purchases in the app and manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Where can I review my progress?

Open Statistics to review your records by period. Return to your daily base in Today and adjust your preferences in Profile.

Can I connect SIGUE to ChatGPT?

The ChatGPT connection is not available from this version of the app. You do not need it to use your daily base. If you have an earlier authorization and need help withdrawing it, email privacidad@holasigue.com.

Do I need to create an account?

Basic personal features work without an account. A SIGUE account is required to subscribe to Premium. Juntos is optional and requires a SIGUE account using Apple, Google, or email to share progress in groups.

How do I connect activity and rest data?

Open Profile and go to Activity and Rest. Before you grant permission, SIGUE explains which data it will read from Apple Health. You can change those permissions in Health or Settings.

Can SIGUE read workouts from my watch?

Yes. If your watch or its app saves compatible workouts to Apple Health, SIGUE can read them with your permission and show them alongside sessions you add manually.

Can I edit an imported session?

You can review and adjust the information SIGUE uses without changing the original record stored in Apple Health.

Why isn't my widget showing an updated summary?

Open SIGUE to refresh the summary and make sure the widget is added from the widget gallery. iOS determines when some background updates happen.

How do I revoke permissions?

You can manage Health, Motion, notifications, and Live Activities from Profile or in iOS Settings.

How do I delete my data?

To delete your Juntos account, go to Profile > SIGUE Account > Delete Account and confirm. Your sessions are revoked and your profile and social progress are deleted; if you manage groups or challenges, you must choose to transfer or delete them. The process may remain pending while Apple confirms revocation. Deleting the app does not delete this account. Your personal records are stored locally and managed separately; original Apple Health data is managed in Health.

Does SIGUE provide medical advice?

No. SIGUE is a general wellbeing app and does not replace evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment by healthcare professionals.

Didn't find what you need?

Tell us what happened, which iPhone or iPad model you use, and, if possible, attach a screenshot without sensitive information.
