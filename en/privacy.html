Summary

SIGUE's personal features work without an account and save your records on your device and, if enabled, in your private iCloud. If you use Juntos, a SIGUE account is created and the information you choose to share in groups is sent to the server. If you authorize the optional ChatGPT connection, SIGUE also stores limited, temporary copies for authorized queries and processes the changes you request through that connection, as explained in section 6.

1. Information stored on your device

SIGUE may store information you enter or configure locally, including:

  • your name and personal focus;
  • habits, intentions, and preferences;
  • eating and rest records;
  • movement and exercise sessions;
  • daily progress, history, and check-ins;
  • notification, appearance, and widget preferences.

This information is used to provide the app's features and personalize your experience on your device.

2. Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, SIGUE may request permission to read steps, walking or running distance, exercise time, sleep, and workouts. This data is used to show your progress and avoid asking you to record an activity twice.

The connection is optional. SIGUE does not write information to Apple Health. You can review or revoke permissions at any time in the Health app or Settings.

3. Motion and Fitness

When you explicitly start a movement session, SIGUE may use Motion and Fitness on iPhone to count the steps taken during that session. The app does not start sessions in the background without your action.

4. Widgets, Live Activities, and notifications

The widget receives a limited summary of your daily baseline through a private group shared between SIGUE and its extension. Live Activities temporarily show the status of sessions you start. Notifications are scheduled on your device according to your preferences.

5. SIGUE account and Juntos features

Juntos is optional and requires a SIGUE account. Depending on the enabled sign-in method, you can use Apple, Google, or email and password. Alejandro Sojo (SIGUE) processes provider identifiers, email where applicable, protected credentials, your display name, session data, and the groups and challenges you join. For a challenge, the progress you submit, its date, the value you choose to share, and, when applicable, the duration you record are stored.

People in your group can see your display name and the progress shared in the challenge. We do not automatically send your personal habits, Apple Health records, or health samples to Juntos. We do not use this data for advertising or sell it.

The Juntos server is hosted in the United States. Data is transmitted between the app and server over HTTPS. The Apple token needed to revoke access when you delete your account is stored encrypted. The hosting provider may process technical connection data to operate and protect the service.

6. Optional ChatGPT connection

Connecting SIGUE to ChatGPT, an OpenAI service, is optional and requires your authorization. Depending on the permissions granted, SIGUE shares account information, habits and progress, Routine plans, and completed-session summaries with ChatGPT to answer your queries. This may include names, targets, schedules, and activity or rest metrics, including summaries derived from Apple Health. Detailed Health samples, heart rate, notes, and GPS routes are not shared.

If you authorize changes, SIGUE receives and stores requests made through ChatGPT to validate and apply them on your device. Manual confirmation is not necessarily required for each change.

SIGUE keeps temporary read copies that expire seven days after their latest update and are deleted through periodic cleanup. Pending requests expire after seven days; records of requests that are no longer pending are deleted through periodic cleanup once 30 days have passed since their creation. Deleted data may remain temporarily in backups until they rotate.

OpenAI processes the information it receives under its terms and privacy policy, which may involve processing outside your country. Its retention and possible use to improve models depend on the product, plan, and data controls of your ChatGPT account.

You can withdraw access by selecting Disconnect ChatGPT in SIGUE. This revokes authorization, deletes read copies from the primary database, and marks pending requests as expired; it does not undo changes already applied. Disconnecting or deleting your SIGUE account does not delete ChatGPT conversations: manage them through OpenAI’s controls or its privacy portal. For requests about your data in SIGUE, email privacidad@holasigue.com.

7. Data we do not collect

SIGUE does not use advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Personal features do not require an account. Detailed Apple Health samples are read on your device with your permission. If you authorize the ChatGPT connection, the derived summaries described in section 6 may be shared.

8. Website

holasigue.com does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tools. The ChatGPT connection uses technical cookies to manage the authorization session. The website stores your language preference in your browser and loads Figtree from Google Fonts; if you use Google or Apple sign-in, it also loads the corresponding provider’s identity services. These connections allow the provider to receive technical data such as your IP address and browser information. The hosting provider may process technical data required to operate the site, such as IP addresses and security logs, under its own obligations and policies.

9. Deletion and control

You can request account deletion in SIGUE > Profile > SIGUE Account > Delete Account. If you manage groups or challenges, you must choose to transfer or delete them. For Apple-linked accounts, the process requires revoking the Apple credential; if that fails, deletion may not complete and you will need to retry. Once the request completes successfully, the account and related data are deleted, including the profile photo, ChatGPT authorizations, read copies, and remote action records. If you cannot access the app, email privacidad@holasigue.com.

Deleted data may remain temporarily in backups until they rotate. You can send access, correction, or deletion requests to the same address.

Deleting the app does not delete your Juntos account or data on the server. It also does not delete local data synced through your private iCloud account. Original Apple Health data is managed in the Health app.

You can revoke Health, Motion, notification, and Live Activity permissions in iOS. We will handle access, correction, or deletion requests under applicable law.

10. Wellbeing, not medical care

SIGUE is a general wellbeing and personal progress tool. It does not provide diagnoses, treatments, or medical recommendations and does not replace care from healthcare professionals.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when SIGUE's features or applicable requirements change. We will publish the current version on this page and indicate its last updated date.

12. Data controller and contact

Alejandro Sojo is responsible for SIGUE. For privacy questions or requests about your data, contact privacidad@holasigue.com. The specific laws that apply may depend, among other things, on your location and the controller's location.
