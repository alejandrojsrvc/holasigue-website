Share respectfully

Juntos is a supportive space. Do not post harassment, threats, hate, explicit sexual content, spam, deceptive links or contact details. Names and descriptions are filtered before publication or editing. Icons and avatars come from a closed catalogue; personal photos are not published.

Report and block

You can report participants, groups and practices through their safety options, with an optional comment of up to 1,000 characters. The reporter’s identity is not shared with the reported person. Check the status under Profile → Juntos safety. A report does not automatically suspend anyone.

Shared spaces

Blocking preserves your membership in existing shared spaces, while mutually hiding profiles, progress and activity and preventing interactions, new overlapping invitations and transfers between both people. Joint summaries that could reveal progress are hidden. Unblocking does not restore deleted interactions or override a block in the opposite direction. Push notifications already delivered to other devices cannot be recalled.

Human review and appeals

Reports are reviewed through a private panel. Our internal goal is to review the queue daily and provide initial attention within 24 hours. We may remove text, suspend or restore Juntos access, archive spaces or propose an ownership transfer. Decisions are recorded. For support or appeals, use the support form or email soporte@holasigue.com.

Ownership and archiving

Each practice has its own owner, separate from the group owner. A transfer requires explicit acceptance and expires after seven days. Receiving an existing space does not require Premium. Leaving or deleting an account archives spaces you still own unless a transfer has already been accepted. Archiving closes new records, edits and invitations while allowing access to your own history and the option to leave.

Suspension

Suspension affects Juntos. Personal features, subscriptions, support and account deletion remain available. Spaces belonging to a suspended owner are paused and read-only; their dates and configuration do not change. Restoring the owner does not reopen archived or removed content.

Data and deletion

Account deletion removes the profile, shared records, interactions and personal text. Archiving retains permitted data belonging to other participants. Personal content or historical text with uncertain authorship is replaced with neutral wording where a space still needs a label. External cleanup is retried persistently and its status can be checked. Deleting your account does not cancel your Apple subscription.

Retention

Resolved reports and their decisions are purged after 90 days. Deleting an account removes its associated personal data and evidence, without exceptional retention by default. Only a minimal receipt and any necessary encrypted credential remain while external cleanup is pending. Backups expire through their rotation.

Acceptance and contact

This version is 2026-11-08. Explicit acceptance of the current rules is required to publish or interact. Reporting, blocking, leaving and deleting an account remain available without accepting a new version. These rules do not replace Apple’s standard EULA for purchases.
