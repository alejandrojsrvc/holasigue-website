This notice supplements the Privacy Policy and explains how data you choose to share in Juntos is handled.

Last updated

November 9, 2026

Controller and scope

Alejandro, in Buenos Aires, Argentina, responsible for SIGUE, offers Juntos as an optional feature for adults. This notice describes the categories of health and wellbeing data that may be processed when you use it. Your personal records are saved on the device.

What data and why

When you voluntarily join a challenge, the server receives the progress you submit: its date, an optional value or duration, participation status, and your profile display name. Challenge activity may reveal information about routines or wellbeing. It is used only to show group progress and statistics and to operate the feature.

Detailed Apple Health samples are processed on your device and are not sent to Juntos. The ChatGPT connection is not available from this iOS version. For information about derived data shared through earlier authorizations, see the earlier ChatGPT authorizations section.

Consent and choice

Juntos is optional. You choose whether to create an account, join a group, and submit records. Practice participants can see your display name, catalogue avatar and shared progress, subject to blocking and moderation. Linking a habit allows its daily values to be sent. Values derived from Apple Health require your explicit permission for that practice; you can withdraw it by editing the link or disconnecting the habit. You can stop recording progress, leave a challenge, or delete your account.

Sharing and location

Progress data is shown only to participants in the relevant practice, subject to blocking and moderation. SIGUE does not sell this data or use it for advertising. The service is hosted on a server in the United States; the infrastructure provider processes data needed to host and protect the service.

Retention and deletion

Leaving or disconnecting a habit stops future submissions; previously shared records may remain while the practice exists. Archiving stops changes and invitations. Account deletion removes your profile, records, interactions and personal text from the primary database, including ChatGPT authorizations and read copies. Spaces you still own are archived unless a transfer has already been accepted, without waiting for another person to respond. The API attempts to finish Apple revocation and storage cleanup in the same request; it only confirms completion once they finish. If they cannot finish, the job is retained for retry and its status is available from Profile. Personal habits, sessions and health information remain on the device. To erase this local data, choose Delete App when removing SIGUE; Offload App keeps the data. Original Apple Health data is managed in Health; older backups are managed in iOS backup settings. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Deleted data may remain temporarily in backups until they rotate.

Start deletion in SIGUE > Profile > Delete SIGUE account. To request access, correction, or deletion, email privacidad@holasigue.com.

Contact

For questions about this notice or how this data is handled, contact Alejandro, in Buenos Aires, Argentina, at privacidad@holasigue.com.
